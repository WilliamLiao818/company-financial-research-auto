# Data card

- Supported source families: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filings and Company Facts; normalized Yahoo Finance annual-statement endpoints used for the expanded prebuilt universe; dated public market-share and target-price sources.
- Prebuilt coverage: 48 U.S.-listed companies in compute and chip design, foundry/equipment/EDA, memory and storage, networking and optics, servers/power/cooling, and cloud and AI platforms. Each company has a specific research role. Reported financials cover the consolidated issuer.
- Focused catalog reviewed 2026-11-22. The previous public catalog was expanded on 2026-10-29. Ticker/CIK identities were checked against the SEC's current company ticker directory. Financial and market-data rows retain their own period dates; catalog expansion does not change the dates of existing observations. A separate native-currency latest-quarter card is shown only where an explicit verified quarterly snapshot and official filing or SEC-filed earnings exhibit are available; it is never inferred from annual data.
- Unit: U.S. dollars unless a row explicitly identifies another currency. Filing-derived rows retain the filed or disclosed convenience-translation basis; provider-normalized rows retain the provider currency code. TSMC FY2025 uses the USD convenience translation in its 20-F; the issuer's reporting currency remains TWD.
- Public input: the company selector and ticker links resolve to the focused catalog. Users can vary scenario assumptions in the browser.
- Processing: the preferred supported standard US-GAAP tag is selected first; within a tag and period, the latest-filed annual fact is retained. Latest-quarter values require an explicit 70–110-day statement column and are not mixed into or annualized within the annual series.
- Provenance: the bundled display snapshot retains filed dates and filing URLs. SEC refresh outputs additionally preserve CIK, selected XBRL tags, accessions, forms and Company Facts URLs when available. Provider rows carry statement-period and source-family metadata.
- Derived metrics: growth, margins, simplified free cash flow, capex intensity, cash conversion and a liabilities/assets proxy are deterministic calculations.
- Scenario inputs: growth and entry/exit multiples are user assumptions and are never presented as sourced company facts. Dated institutional targets remain separate from The Company Bear/Base/Bull range.
- Added-company research: company-specific business exposures, growth drivers, KPI checklists, risks and selected peer context are qualitative research frameworks. Their sector scenario defaults are editable stress tests. No unreviewed numerical competitive ratings, market shares or target prices are added. Peers may be value-chain counterparts, not direct competitors.
- Missingness policy: unsupported or absent facts remain null; the connector does not estimate, interpolate or silently substitute company-specific values.
- Public research uses issuer-level information. Scenario inputs apply to the user's session.
- Limitations: company-specific tags, restatements, fiscal calendars, foreign-issuer reporting currencies and provider normalization may affect comparability. Foreign-issuer market series represent the specified U.S.-traded shares or ADRs, while financial statements can remain in a different reporting currency. Corporate separations can limit comparisons with predecessor statements.
- License/usage: public regulatory filings; this repository stores a normalized factual snapshot and source URLs.
